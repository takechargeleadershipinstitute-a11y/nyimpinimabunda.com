The Story

From a village in Gazankuluto the chairman's seat.

He was fifteen when he matriculated, nineteen when he walked into a multinational, and twenty-one when he first led a team. Four companies mark the road from there to the chair.

15 Gazankulu Matriculates at fifteen A village school in an apartheid-era homeland. At nineteen he joins Procter & Gamble; at twenty-one he leads his first team.
2006 Nando's Leaves the corporate world to run a cluster of franchises as owner-operator.
2013 Diageo CEO of Uganda Breweries. His first executive-suite role, in a country not his own.
2016 Vodacom CEO, Consumer Business. Then Senior Advisor to BCG across sub-Saharan Africa.
2020 General Electric CEO, GE Southern Africa. CEO of an international company before forty; a year later, Chair of the U.S.–South Africa Business Council.
Now The chair Chairperson, Schindler South Africa Board chair. Non-executive director. Executive coach. Host of CEO Nights. The climb is finished. The work now is lifting other people up it.

The Institute

Africa's leadership institute, built on seven guiding areas that together develop leaders, tell their stories, and open the door for the next generation.

What is TCLI?

The Take Charge Leadership Institute is the home of Nyimpini Mabunda's leadership work, bringing his keynotes, coaching, teaching, writing and broadcasting together under one roof, so that what he has learned on the road to CEO is available to the people climbing it now.

Why TCLI?

Africa does not have a shortage of talent. It has a shortage of access: to the mentorship, the rooms and the plain instruction that turn capable people into leaders. Too many join organisations full of promise and quietly disappear inside them. The Institute exists to close that gap.

Purpose

To develop the next generation of African leaders, equipping them with the capability, the confidence and the networks to lead at the highest level, and to bring others with them when they get there.

The Book
