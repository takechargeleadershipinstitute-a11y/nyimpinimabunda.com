The programme

Higher education,meet industry.

U-GRIP creates a practical learning experience that equips graduates with the knowledge, confidence and networks required for long-term career success.

Higher education

Graduates with qualifications, ready to put them to work.

U-GRIP

Programme vision

To become South Africa's leading graduate employability platform, connecting higher education with industry while preparing graduates for the future economy.

Programme mission

To equip graduates with practical workplace knowledge, future-ready skills, leadership capability and access to employment opportunities through strategic partnerships between universities, industry and government.
